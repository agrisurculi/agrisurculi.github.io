- Request -

The client requested a simple, straight forward yet elegant app for demostrating his new launched AR cardboard headset. Consistency with the company branding is important to the client.



- Project Scope -

- Design a easy-to-navigate app experience

- Allow easy incorporation to future demo upadtes and potentially feature holokit users' works

- Study company branding style and design the visuals accordinglly


- Research -

Brand

The company Amber Garage has a distinct style. The goal is to keep consistent.


Target Audiences

The targets are Holokit users which means AR developer, AR artists, AR enthusiasts. These people usually has some level of experiences with AR and mainly looking for a quick demostration for the hardware.



- Flow -

I started to sketch out the flow and finished it in Sketch.


The walk through after launching a project is very important for this app. It is crucial for the instructions to be very easy to follow so that people don't get confused.


- Visual -

As of UI, I paid special attention to the details like color use, layout composition and fonts.


- Communication -

I worked not only with the client but also the developer, so communication needs to be precise in order to produce an accurate outcome.
